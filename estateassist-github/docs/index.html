A few good questions

Before we
get started.

Have a question that is not here? Use the form and tell us what you are exploring.

Ask about your workflow ↗
Does EstateAssist show live listings or availability?

Not in this demo. A client deployment would only show property details supplied and approved by that client. Availability and pricing should be confirmed by the agency team.

Can the assistant answer any question?

It should answer from the business’s approved information. If it cannot find a reliable answer, it should say so and offer a human follow-up.

How does lead consent work?

The assistant asks before collecting contact details. The client must decide what information is necessary, how it is stored, who can access it and how long it is retained.

Can it book property visits automatically?

The demo only records a preferred time as a request. A real appointment is not confirmed until an authorised team member checks availability and responds.

What does setup involve?

We would map the enquiry process, agree approved answers and handoff rules, configure the flow, and review it with your team before any live use.

Is the pricing final?

No. The figures above are proposed introductory demo pricing only. Scope, software requirements and any ongoing costs would be agreed before a client engagement.

Demo records stay on this computer.

A real service would require an approved privacy notice, retention policy, secure storage and access controls before collecting prospect information.
